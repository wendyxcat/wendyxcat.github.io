My Favourite City: Victoria

Victoria, British Columbia is the capital city of British Columbia, Canada, located on the southern tip of Vancouver Island. It is known for British colonial-era charm,gardens, and coastal setting.

The city is very nice for living. Commonly known as the "Garden City" due to its lush parks, gardens, and thousands of hanging floral baskets.

It is bordered by the Juan de Fuca Strait and Haro Strait to the south and east, looking across to the Olympic Mountains in Washington State (USA) and the Gulf Islands. It lies about 100 kilometers southwest of Vancouver across the Salish Sea.

About the City

Victoria's landscape is defined by an irregular, dramatic coastline of rocky shores, sheltered coves, and deep natural inlets—most famously the Inner Harbour right in the downtown core.

It is a great place to explore.
